{% translate "How to add a passkey on another device" %}
  1. {% translate "On the other phone or computer, open this portal and sign in with an existing passkey. If offered by your browser, use your phone or another device to sign in." %}
  2. {% translate "Open Profile, then choose Add a passkey on another device. You do not need to change or select your sign-in method again." %}
  3. {% translate "Confirm with an existing passkey. Enter a recognizable name for the additional passkey, then choose Create passkey. In the browser prompt, choose where to save it." %}
  4. {% translate "Choose Save passkey to finish. Your existing passkeys remain usable. Other sessions end and you receive a security notice." %}

{% translate "The available devices and choices depend on your browser and passkey provider. A synchronized passkey may already work on several devices; a device name does not prove a separate backup." %}
